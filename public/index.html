🎵 Miku Monday Bot 🎵

Spreading Hatsune Miku joy every Monday!

Open in Telegram Bot status

About

The Miku Monday Bot automatically sends a Hatsune Miku GIF every Monday at 12:00 AM GMT+8 (4:00 PM UTC Sunday) to the Telegram channels it's added to, plus a daily hype message at 12:00 AM GMT+8 with a Vocaloid song of the day.

Features

  • Sends a Miku GIF every Monday at 12:00 AM GMT+8 (4:00 PM UTC Sunday)
  • Sends daily hype messages at 12:00 AM GMT+8 to build anticipation
  • Works with multiple public or private Telegram channels
  • No technical setup required for end users
  • Completely free and open source

How to use

Add it to your Telegram channels, or have the bot DM you:

  1. Search for @itsmikumondaybot in Telegram, or open t.me/itsmikumondaybot
  2. Click "Start" or send /start
  3. Add the bot to your Telegram channels as an administrator
  4. Send /start@itsmikumondaybot to register your channel to the bot

Commands

  • /start Welcome message and instructions
  • /help Show help information
  • /status Bot status, subscription info and next scheduled post
  • /countdown Time remaining until the next Miku Monday
  • /today Today's daily hype message
  • /unsubscribe Remove this channel from bot subscriptions
  • /feedback Send feedback to the developer (@JulianC97)

Tips

  • Make sure the bot has permission to post messages in your channels
  • The bot works in both public and private channels, and in as many as you like
  • Not receiving daily messages? Check the channel is registered with /status
